Agriculture Tips

  • Prepare paddy nurseries

    With the northeast monsoon onset, farmers in the Cauvery delta should prepare paddy nursery beds and ensure drainage channels are clear of debris.

  • Groundnut harvest window

    Dry spells between monsoon episodes are ideal for groundnut harvesting. Monitor weather forecasts daily and aim to complete harvesting before the next rain cycle.

  • Protect maize from wind damage

    Stake tall maize varieties to prevent lodging during strong wind events. Apply potassium-rich fertilizers to strengthen stalk tissue before heavy rain periods.

  • Irrigate banana plantations carefully

    Banana crops are sensitive to waterlogging. Ensure ridges are raised and furrow irrigation is employed to prevent root rot during high-rainfall months.

  • Apply organic mulch to vegetable beds

    A 5–8 cm layer of paddy straw mulch conserves soil moisture, moderates soil temperature, and suppresses weed growth during the onset of the dry season.

  • Millet cultivation opportunity in rain-shadow zones

    Farmers in rain-shadow districts should sow finger millet or pearl millet, which require less water and can withstand short dry spells effectively.

  • Integrated pest management post-monsoon

    Post-monsoon moisture creates favourable conditions for fungal diseases. Apply copper-based fungicides and monitor crops for blast, blight, and rust infections.
